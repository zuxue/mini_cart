# Payment icon sources

- `payment-wechat.png`: WeChat Pay official brand logo from the WeChat Pay documentation (`wxpaylogo_xxxhdpi.png`).
- `payment-alipay.png`: Alipay logo from the image referenced by the official Alipay website.
- `payment-unionpay.png`: UnionPay APP mark from the official UnionPay International artwork download center (`UnionPayAPP.rar`).
- `payment-balance.png`: `wallet-cards` icon from the official Lucide open-source icon repository.

The source artwork is only cropped, padded, and resized for consistent rendering in mini programs. Brand colors and shapes are unchanged.
